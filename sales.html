RefreshU EssentialOrganised, flexible coordination

For independent travellers who want the important details organised while keeping their flexibility.

  • Portal access, onboarding and non-medical coordination
  • Referral to an independent physician and coordination of the review
  • Personalised travel timeline and arrival planning
  • Flight-itinerary coordination for appointments
  • Hotel and ground-transportation recommendations
  • Travel-readiness checklist and insurance status tracking
  • Pre-departure concierge call and destination guide
RefreshU SignatureArrival to departure

Everything in Essential, plus the full coordinated stay.

  • Airport pickup and private ground transportation
  • Hotel selection and reservation assistance
  • A local concierge point of contact
  • Restaurants, grocery, laundry, barber, nails and wellness
  • Pharmacy pickup for physician-directed items
  • Welcome kit and recovery-logistics coordination
  • Daily non-medical check-in
Most complete
RefreshU White GloveDedicated concierge

Everything in Signature, plus premium personalised support.

  • A dedicated RefreshU concierge
  • Premium or private transportation throughout
  • Premium hotel or suite and room preparation
  • Barber, beauty and wellness to the hotel
  • Personal shopping, errands, companion itinerary
  • Non-medical translation assistance

Say on the call: a dedicated concierge is a hospitality and logistics professional, not a private nurse or healthcare professional.

RefreshU Companion & FamilyTwo or more travelling

For couples, friends or family, whether one person or several are seeing providers.

  • One coordinated group itinerary and shared transfers
  • Accommodation planning for the group
  • Separate physician coordination per adult
  • Companion transportation, activities and itinerary
  • Babysitting and childcare vendor coordination

Each adult receiving medical services completes their own physician review, consents, provider agreement and medical payment. One person’s physician decision never applies to another traveller.

RefreshU BespokeQuoted individually

When travel, hospitality, companion or lifestyle requirements go beyond the standard packages.

  • Luxury hotel or suite, chauffeur, executive transfers
  • Private guide, private chef, personal stylist
  • Fine dining and special reservations
  • Extended concierge support and extended stay

Quote individually and show it in the Service Summary before purchase. Never improvise a price on a call.

Never part of a package, and never quoted as one. Physician consultation, evaluation and candidacy, the procedure, anesthesia, hospital and facility services, laboratory and diagnostic testing, interpretation of results, prescriptions, clinical nursing, medical clearance, informed consent, aftercare and emergency services. These are the physician’s or the clinic’s, charged separately and paid to them. A single combined total would make RefreshU look like the seller of medical care.
