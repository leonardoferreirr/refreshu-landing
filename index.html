Five levels of concierge support. Medical procedures and medical services are never part of a RefreshU package: they are provided and charged separately by independent physicians and clinics.

For independent travellers who want the important details organised while keeping their flexibility.

  • Client portal access, onboarding and non-medical coordination
  • Referral to a participating independent physician, and coordination of the review workflow
  • Personalised travel timeline and arrival planning
  • Flight-itinerary coordination for scheduled appointments
  • Hotel and ground-transportation recommendations
  • Travel-readiness checklist, document reminders and insurance status tracking
  • Pre-departure concierge call and a digital destination guide
Most complete

RefreshU Signature

The full experience, coordinated from arrival to departure. Everything in Essential, plus:

  • Airport pickup and private ground transportation
  • Hotel selection and reservation assistance
  • A local RefreshU concierge point of contact
  • Restaurant selection, grocery, laundry, barber, nails and wellness coordination
  • Pharmacy pickup for items your physician directed. RefreshU does not select or prescribe medication
  • RefreshU welcome kit and recovery-logistics coordination
  • Daily non-medical check-in during the support period

RefreshU White Glove

Our highest level of personalised support. Everything in Signature, plus:

  • A dedicated RefreshU concierge
  • Premium or private transportation throughout the service period
  • Premium hotel or suite assistance and pre-arrival room preparation
  • Barber, beauty and wellness professionals to the hotel where available
  • Personal shopping, errands and a companion or family itinerary
  • Non-medical translation and communication assistance

A RefreshU dedicated concierge is a non-clinical hospitality and logistics professional, and is not a private nurse or healthcare professional.

RefreshU Companion & Family

For couples, friends or family travelling together, whether one person or several are seeing participating providers.

  • One coordinated group itinerary and shared airport transportation
  • Accommodation planning for the group
  • Separate physician coordination for each adult receiving medical services
  • Companion transportation, activities and tourist itinerary
  • Babysitting and childcare vendor coordination where available

Each person receiving medical services completes their own physician review, provider documentation, medical consents, provider agreement and medical payment. One person’s physician decision does not apply to another traveller.

RefreshU Bespoke

A fully customised experience when travel, hospitality, companion or lifestyle requirements go beyond the standard packages.

  • Luxury hotel or suite, chauffeur and executive airport transportation
  • Private guide, private chef, personal stylist and personal shopping
  • Fine-dining and special reservations, customised city experiences
  • Extended dedicated concierge support and extended stay coordination

Bespoke services are quoted individually and shown in your Service Summary before purchase.

What is never part of a RefreshU package

Physician consultation, medical evaluation and determination of candidacy, surgery or procedure, anesthesia or sedation, hospital and facility services, laboratory or diagnostic testing, interpretation of results, prescriptions and medication selection, clinical nursing, medical clearance, clinical informed consent, medical aftercare and management of complications, and emergency medical services. These are provided and charged separately by independent healthcare professionals.

Your destination

RefreshU operates in two Brazilian cities. You choose the one your stay is built around, and the concierge experience is the same in both.

The country’s medical capital. The largest concentration of clinics, hospitals and specialists in Latin America, in a city built for people who came to work and stayed for the food.

Neighbourhoods we place you in
Jardins, Itaim Bibi, Vila Nova Conceição and Alto de Pinheiros. Walkable, residential, and close to the clinics.
What the city is known for
Michelin dining, Ibirapuera park, MASP, the Oscar Freire shopping district, and the São Roque wine route an hour away.
Best suited to
A stay centred on the procedure and on recovering in comfort, with a dense, urban week around it.

    Questions people ask first

    Who decides whether I am a candidate?

    A participating physician does, after reviewing your questionnaire and photos. RefreshU coordinates the journey and never makes the medical decision.

    Is this medical tourism?

    Not in the way that phrase is normally used. RefreshU is a concierge service built around a physician led journey, where the trip is designed rather than improvised.

    How much will I save?

    It depends on the procedure and on what you compare it to. We publish comparisons only where the figures and the methodology can be verified and approved.

    Can someone travel with me?

    Yes. Packages support a partner, a friend or family, and your companion can look into a treatment of their own if they want to.

    What happens to my medical information?

    It is collected through a private questionnaire and sent for review by the physician handling your case.

    What about travel insurance?

    Delays and cancellations can affect the medical schedule, and real coverage depends on each individual policy. RefreshU does not promise reimbursement.
